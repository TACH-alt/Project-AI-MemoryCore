## TikTok structured fallback upgrade

- Ran deeper TikTok extraction research and verified that the main failure pattern was not always a broken downloader. Some public TikTok links are genuinely photo-mode posts, so `yt-dlp` only exposes `audio` while the page still exposes usable image and music assets.
- Added a structured TikTok fallback in `AI - Shoppee Video v1.0/scripts/process_shopee_video.py`:
  - parse `__UNIVERSAL_DATA_FOR_REHYDRATION__`
  - classify TikTok post as direct video or photo-mode
  - recover the best direct video URL when present
  - otherwise recover one best photo asset plus direct music/audio URL
- Fixed two real regressions during the upgrade:
  - `audio/mp4` TikTok music URLs without extensions were being treated incorrectly until content-type mapping was expanded
  - a rename helper could delete an already-correct photo asset and leave only a dead path in memory
- Tightened rerun behavior for photo-mode rows:
  - clear stale raw image artifacts before redownload
  - clear stale edited mp4 outputs before rerender
  - keep canonical naming stable
