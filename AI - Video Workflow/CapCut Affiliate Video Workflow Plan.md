# CapCut Affiliate Video Workflow Plan

**Project**: AI - Video Workflow  
**Date**: 2026-04-23  
**Use Case**: Shopee and TikTok affiliate product videos  
**Planning Status**: Draft workflow and cost estimate  
**Recommended Direction**: Hosted CapCut/Pippit workflow first, local automation later

## Goal

Build a repeatable workflow for creating short affiliate product videos where the video creation and editing layer uses CapCut or CapCut-powered tools.

The target output is short vertical videos for:

- TikTok affiliate product posts
- TikTok Shop product videos
- Shopee Video
- Reels or Shorts reposts when useful

## Recommended Workflow Summary

Use **Pippit AI / CapCut Commerce Pro** for product-link-to-video generation and **CapCut** for final manual editing.

This gives the fastest path because Pippit is designed for e-commerce content and CapCut is strong for short-form editing, captions, templates, audio, and vertical export.

## Main Workflow

### 1. Product Selection

Pick one product from Shopee or TikTok Shop.

Capture:

- Product URL
- Affiliate URL
- Product name
- Price
- Discount
- Product images
- Product rating
- Top review highlights
- Common customer pain point
- Best visual benefit

### 2. Script and Angle

Generate 3 versions:

- Problem-solution angle
- Demo/result angle
- Deal/urgency angle

Each script should have:

- Hook
- Problem
- Product reveal
- Demo or proof
- Benefit
- CTA

Recommended video length:

- 20-35 seconds for fast affiliate posts
- 35-60 seconds only when the product needs explanation

### 3. Generate Base Video in Pippit

Use Pippit / CapCut Commerce Pro:

1. Paste product link or upload product visuals.
2. Select product video or marketing video workflow.
3. Generate several video options.
4. Pick the best version.
5. Export or continue editing.

Pippit publicly describes features including:

- Link to Video
- AI Product Images
- AI Avatars and Voices
- Scheduling and Auto-Publishing
- Analytics
- Add TikTok Shop links to videos
- Commercial templates/assets, with licensing details per asset/template

Sources:

- [Pippit AI homepage](https://www.pippit.ai/)
- [Pippit AI pricing](https://www.pippit.ai/pricing)

### 4. Final Edit in CapCut

Use CapCut desktop or mobile for final polish:

- Set format to 9:16 vertical.
- Add or clean auto-captions.
- Add hook text in the first 1-2 seconds.
- Add product name and price overlay.
- Add simple benefit bullets.
- Add CTA near the end.
- Add affiliate disclosure in caption or video text if needed.
- Use commercial-safe music/audio.
- Export at 1080x1920.

Recommended export:

```text
Resolution: 1080x1920
Aspect ratio: 9:16
Frame rate: 30 fps
Format: MP4 / H.264
Length: 20-35 seconds
```

### 5. Publish

For TikTok:

- Upload the final video.
- Add affiliate product link / TikTok Shop product.
- Add disclosure when required.
- Use 3-6 relevant hashtags.
- Track views, clicks, orders, and commission.

For Shopee:

- Upload to Shopee Video if available.
- Use Shopee affiliate link in supported destinations.
- Repurpose video for TikTok, Reels, Shorts, Telegram, or Facebook where allowed.

### 6. Track Performance

Track every video in `Product Video Tracker.md`.

## Content Production Target

### First 14 Days

- Create 10 product videos.
- Test 2-3 niches.
- Use 3 hook styles per niche.
- Post consistently.
- Track basic metrics.

### First 30 Days

- Create 20-30 product videos.
- Identify top 5 products.
- Identify top 3 hook formats.
- Repeat winning formats with new products.
- Decide whether Pippit Starter is worth paying for.

## Recommended Video Template

```text
0-2s: Hook
2-6s: Problem
6-12s: Product reveal
12-22s: Demo / proof / use case
22-30s: Benefit + CTA
```

Example:

```text
Hook:
Kalau meja selalu bersepah, benda kecil ni boleh bantu.

Problem:
Remote, cable, charger semua selalu hilang bila nak guna.

Product reveal:
Ini organizer kecil yang boleh lekat tepi meja.

Demo:
Letak remote, cable, earphone, semua terus ada tempat.

Benefit:
Meja nampak kemas dan barang senang cari.

CTA:
Saya letak link dekat sini. Kalau tengah promo, boleh grab sekarang.
```

## Compliance Checklist

Before publishing:

- [ ] Product claim is truthful.
- [ ] No fake before/after result.
- [ ] No copyrighted video copied from another creator.
- [ ] Affiliate/commercial relationship is disclosed where required.
- [ ] Product link matches the actual product shown.
- [ ] No misleading "free", "guaranteed", or fake urgency claims.
- [ ] Music/template asset is safe for commercial use.

TikTok branded content policy includes content where the creator receives commission through affiliate links or promotional codes.

Source: [TikTok Branded Content Policy](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/global/bc-policy/en)

TikTok Shop affiliate videos used in ads require creator authorization.

Source: [TikTok affiliate creatives for Shop Ads](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/affiliate-creative-videos-in-shop-ads?lang=en)
